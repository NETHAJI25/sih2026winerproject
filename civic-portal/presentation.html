Problem Statement

The Civic Reporting Gap

Problem Statement SIH25031 — Crowdsourced Civic Issue Reporting and Resolution System

  • 1No easy channel for citizens to report potholes, garbage dumps, broken streetlights, water leaks or other civic problems quickly.
  • 2Unstructured data — government departments do not receive structured, geotagged, photo-backed reports they can act upon.
  • 3Issues get ignored — no ownership, no deadlines, no escalation, and no feedback loop to the citizen who reported.
  • 4Zero transparency — citizens cannot track the status of their complaint, eroding trust in civic agencies.
  • 5No accountability — departments have no analytics or priority data to allocate resources where they matter most.

Our Solution

Two Portals, One Workflow

A citizen portal for reporting and a government portal for resolution, linked by a shared data layer.

1Citizen Portal

Report issues with a photo and GPS location in under a minute. Track every status update, from Submitted to Resolved.

2Government Portal

Officers view structured, geotagged reports, update statuses, and act on prioritized, category-tagged workloads.

3Geotagged Photo Reports

Every issue is mapped, photographed and categorized, giving departments verified, actionable evidence.

4Analytics Dashboard

Live charts show issue density by area, resolution rates and department workloads for data-driven decisions.
