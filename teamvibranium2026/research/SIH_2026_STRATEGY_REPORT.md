## 2. How SIH selection actually works (the facts)

| Metric | SIH 2024 | SIH 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Total problem statements | 254 | 271 |
| Idea submissions | ~49,000 teams | 72,165 |
| Grand finale teams | ~1,700 | 1,360 |
| Winner slots | 315 | 321 |
| 1st prize | ₹1,00,000 | **₹1,50,000** |
| Joint winner prize | ₹50,000 | ₹75,000 |

**Key rule:** Winning happens **per Problem Statement (PS)**, not per domain. Each PS gets 1-2 winners. So you don't win "the health domain" — you win *your specific PS*. Choice of PS decides your odds.

## 5. Top recommendation: **SIH25065 — Roof-Top Rainwater Harvesting & Artificial Recharge Assessment App** (Ministry of Jal Shakti)

**Why it's the strongest pick for SIH 2026:**
1. **National priority** — NITI Aayog: 21 major Indian cities will run out of groundwater by 2030. This directly serves PM Modi's Jal Shakti Abhiyan (flagship, visible to judges).
2. **Jal Shakti is a proven winning ministry** — 26 winners in SIH 2024 (2nd highest), 12 in 2025.
3. **Low competition** — 267 national submissions (47% below cap) → best odds.
4. **Pure software, demoable in days** — select rooftop on map → auto-calc catchment area → rainfall data → harvesting potential + recharge pit sizing (real hydrology formulas). No hardware risk.
5. **Viable product** — rainwater harvesting is **legally mandated** in 18+ states; usable by Jal Shakti Abhiyan, urban bodies, builders, citizens.

**Alternates:** SIH25092 (Digital Mental Health — huge impact, but 500 competitors), SIH25071 (Rockfall prediction — impressive but hard to demo in 3 days).
