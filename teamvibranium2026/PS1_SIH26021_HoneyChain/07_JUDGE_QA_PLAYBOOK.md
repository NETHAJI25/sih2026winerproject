## Killer questions (guaranteed)

**Q1: "Doesn't Madhukranti already do blockchain traceability?"**
A: "Madhukranti registers actors and issues IDs — 16,788 beekeepers, 24 lakh colonies. It's registration infrastructure. It doesn't reach the retail jar, the consumer can't verify anything, and there's no apiary management layer. We integrate with their registry and close the loop to the last mile."
Backup: NBHM objective (d) itself calls for developing blockchain traceability — we're executing stated policy intent.

**Q2: "Why blockchain and not a normal database?"**
A: "Because a database is owned by one party and editable by its admin. When the asset is trust itself — export compliance, adulteration disputes — no single party, including us or an FPO, can be allowed to rewrite history. Each custody hop is signed by a different org key."
Backup: personal data stays off-chain per DPDP Act; only hashes + events on-chain.

**Q3: "Will a villager actually use this?"**
A: "The app works offline, in Tamil/Hindi, icon-first with voice prompts, on a ₹6,000 phone. Syncs when signal returns. We demo it live with airplane mode."
Backup: beekeeper does 3 actions total per harvest; everything else automatic.

**Q4: "What's your business model / sustainability?"**
A: "SaaS per-batch fee for processors/exporters (they save recall risk), free for beekeepers and FPOs. Government alignment via NBHM's existing FPO network of 97 organizations is the distribution channel."

**Q5: "How do you stop garbage data entering the chain? Blockchain only proves immutability, not truth."**
A: "Correct — oracle problem. Three controls: geo-tagged photo evidence at batch creation, weight-physics checks at every hop (our AI flags impossible mass gains), and lab certificates attached by lab-org keys only."
Backup: this exact question is where most blockchain teams die; our anomaly-alert module is the answer — say that sentence verbatim.

**Q6: "IoT sensors?"**
A: "Roadmap module. Core value needs zero hardware; sensors enhance hive-health analytics later at ~₹3–5k/node."

**Q7: "Cost to run?"**
A: "Ledger testnet now; production consortium nodes shared across FPOs ≈ under ₹1/batch at scale. Hosting free-tier today, ₹2–3k/month pilot."

**Q8: "What if a processor refuses adoption?"**
A: "Export compliance pressure — US/EU buyers increasingly demand lot provenance; APEDA MEP regime shows govt willingness to regulate. And consumers scanning QR create pull-through for brands."
