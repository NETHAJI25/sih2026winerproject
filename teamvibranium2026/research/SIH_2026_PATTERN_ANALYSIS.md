# SIH Winners Pattern Analysis (2025 data → 2026 strategy)

**Data:** Full official SIH 2025 grand-finale dataset — 271 PS, 72,165 national submissions, 1,360 finalist teams, 310 winner/joint-winner slots (`sih2025_full.csv`).

---

## Pattern 1 — Competition level decides your odds (5.5× lever)

| National submissions per PS | # PS | True win rate |
|---|---|---|
| < 150 | 109 | **1.21%** |
| 150–250 | 42 | 0.62% |
| 250–350 | 23 | 0.42% |
| 350–450 | 15 | 0.33% |
| 450–500 (full) | 82 | **0.22%** |

→ A PS that closes at ~100 submissions gives **~5.5× better odds** than a full 500-submission PS. Same work, same judges.

## Pattern 2 — Zero-winner traps (11 PS awarded NO winner in 2025)

- **8 of 11 were ISRO PS** (space-tech research depth students can't fake in 36h).
- Others: non-destructive gold assaying (hardware NDT), radar person-detection under rubble (hardware), AI personalized learning path generator (500 subs, saturated AND quality bar unmet).
- **Rule:** avoid research-grade org PS (ISRO/NTRO-deep-tech), avoid hardware needing lab validation, avoid saturated generic-AI-education topics.

## Pattern 3 — Category

- Hardware true win rate 0.68% vs Software 0.37% — but only pick hardware if you own the hardware. **Your team (Nethaji AI/full-stack, Devraj app dev, 4 × PPT/R&D/pitch) = pure software team. Lock Software.**

## Pattern 5 — Theme supply vs demand

Most winner slots: Smart Automation (33), Agriculture/FoodTech/Rural (31), Smart Education (26), Miscellaneous (25).
Worst efficiency: Space Technology (2.9%), Smart Vehicles (3.4%), Robotics & Drones (6.1%) — high effort, few slots.

## Pattern 6 — Geography

Maharashtra (80) + Tamil Nadu (76) = 41% of all wins. Chennai colleges top the table (CIT 9×, St. Joseph's 6×, SVCE/Sri Sairam/Sri Krishna 4× each). SRM teams winning is normal — the ecosystem is proven.

### Hard rules for selection day
1. **Watch the idea counter on sih.gov.in.** Commit only to a PS tracking toward <250 submissions at deadline. If your pick crosses 350 by mid-window, switch to your #2.
2. Never pick: ISRO/space-research PS (0-for-11 in '25), hardware without hardware members, generic AI-education/chatbot PS (they fill to 500 fastest and still got zeroed once).
3. One PS per team focus — last year you spread across two submissions and hit the file-loss disaster. One PS, three backups (PC + teammate laptop + Drive), rehearsed demo script.
4. Girls' R&D output should feed the exact evaluator rubric: problem quantification (govt statistics), feasibility (working demo video), impact (beneficiary count), scalability (rollout plan). That's what separates the 1 winner from 499 shortlisted.
