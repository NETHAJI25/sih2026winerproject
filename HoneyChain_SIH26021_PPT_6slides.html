How It Works — 5-Step V2 Flow

  1. Farmer: geotagged photo + farmerWeight (42kg) → Batch B-1042 created → pickup alert to lab
  2. Driver: auto-allocated via geo, vehicle scale confirms 41.8kg → on-chain transfer
  3. Lab: received 41.6kg → wastage 3.6 + pure 38kg logged; cert + tester photo uploaded
  4. Packer: received 37.9kg → mints 500 jars → QR = hc.in/b/B-1042 (only packer can mint)
  5. Consumer: scans QR → sees farmer photo, driver, lab cert, packer — physics-checked, flagged if 42→55kg
DB: farmer_weight / lab_* / packer_* | Chain: BatchCreated→Custody×3→Quality→JarsMinted | AI flags impossible gains
